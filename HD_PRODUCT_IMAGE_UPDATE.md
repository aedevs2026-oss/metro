# Metro Furniture — HD Product Image Update

## What changed
- Upscaled all 144 product gallery WebP images to high-resolution display sizes while preserving each image's aspect ratio and existing path.
- Upscaled and gently sharpened 55 additional catalogue, lighting, shop and homepage product images.
- Kept existing filenames and extensions unchanged, so current product/category mappings continue to work.
- Used high-quality Lanczos resampling and restrained sharpening; no product designs or colourways were intentionally changed.

## Image dimensions
- Gallery primary/detail: 2400 × 1800 px
- Gallery wide: 2880 × 1800 px
- Gallery portrait: 2000 × 2500 px
- Catalogue/source images: scaled up to a maximum long edge of approximately 2500 px where needed.

## Important quality note
Upscaling improves display size and perceived sharpness, but it cannot recover true details missing from a low-resolution source. For native 4K product photography, use original high-resolution catalogue/camera files or separately approve generated product views per SKU. Product appearance, crop ratios, file paths and SKU mappings were preserved.
